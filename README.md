## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains interactive financial data models and analytics reports built using **Microsoft Excel**. The objective of this project is to analyze customer profitability, net sales trends, and cost structures to empower data-driven business decisions.

The core analysis evaluates key financial performance drivers across sales channels, platforms (e.g., Amazon), and customer segments.

---

## 📂 Key Reports Included in Repository
* 📄 **[P&L Report (PDF)](P&L.pdf):** Profit and Loss summary breaking down total revenue, cost driver breakdowns, and net profitability.
* 📄 **[Net Sales Performance (PDF)](Net%20sales.pdf):** In-depth analysis of overall revenue generation across timeframes and product lines.
* 📄 **[Customer Net Sales Report (PDF)](Customer%20net%20sales.pdf):** Marketplace and account-level breakdown (Net Sales, COGS, Gross Profit) evaluating profitability by customer account.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Skills & Concepts Applied
* **Financial Modeling & Metrics:** Calculated Net Sales, COGS (Cost of Goods Sold), and Gross Margin percentage across multi-table reports.
* **PivotTables & Data Summarization:** Formatted dynamic dynamic PivotTables with custom layouts to aggregate high-volume transaction data.
* **Formula Architecture & Logic:** Utilized error-handling dynamic array formulas and dynamic lookups to avoid formula errors (`#SPILL!`, `#N/A`).
* **Reporting & Layout Formatting:** Designed print-ready PDF financial statements with clear typography, grid control, and account logo branding.

---

## 💡 Business Insights & Impact
1. **Profitability Tracking:** Identified gross margin variations across key marketplace accounts.
2. **Cost Optimization:** Evaluated the ratio between Net Sales and COGS to highlight products with high profit potential versus low margin drivers.
3. **Executive Reporting:** Built clean, presentation-grade executive PDF summaries for rapid financial review.
